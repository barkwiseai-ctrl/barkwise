BarkWise

Child Safety Standards

Effective date: March 14, 2026

BarkWise is a social app for adult dog owners and local pet service providers. We do not allow content or behavior that exploits, harms, or endangers children.

1. Zero tolerance content policy

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation material (CSAM/CSEM) is strictly prohibited.
  • Any grooming, sexualization, or predatory behavior involving minors is strictly prohibited.
  • Threats, coercion, or abuse involving minors are strictly prohibited.

2. In-app safety and reporting tools

  • Users can report posts/events/users from in-app safety actions.
  • Users can block other users in community and messaging surfaces.
  • Moderation queue and moderator actions are used to review and resolve reports.

3. How to report child safety concerns

  • In app: open safety actions and choose the child safety report option.
  • Email: safety@barkwise.app

If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

4. Enforcement

We may remove content, restrict features, suspend, or permanently remove accounts that violate these standards. We may preserve relevant evidence and cooperate with lawful requests as required by applicable law.
