BarkWise

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BarkWise collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the BarkWise mobile app (package: com.barkwise.app), web experiences, and related services.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account and identity data: user ID, invite email, OTP login metadata.
  • User-generated content: chat messages, profile details, group posts, comments, lost/found posts, booking notes, and direct messages.
  • Media uploads: photos you upload for community/lost-found posts (including filename and content metadata).
  • Location data: approximate location (coarse location permission) and suburb, plus optional latitude/longitude provided in certain flows.
  • Device and notification data: device push token and notification read status.
  • Security and diagnostics data: rate-limit events, failed login counters, moderation reports, and optional analytics/diagnostic events submitted by the app.

2. How We Use Data

  • Provide app features (community, messaging, booking, notifications, and onboarding).
  • Authenticate users and protect against abuse.
  • Personalize recommendations and nearby results.
  • Process and display your community content.
  • Operate and improve AI assistant responses where enabled.

3. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We may share limited data with service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • OpenAI: chat content and related context for AI responses when AI mode is enabled.
  • Resend: invite/OTP email delivery when email OTP is enabled.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notification delivery using device tokens.
  • Google services: maps/location functionality when those features are used.

We do not sell personal data.

4. Data Storage and Retention

BarkWise stores operational data in application databases and file storage. We keep data while your account is active and as needed to run the service, comply with legal obligations, and handle security/abuse issues.

Some records (for example, security logs or backup copies) may remain for a limited period after account deletion.

5. Account Deletion

You can request account deletion from within the app. Our backend supports account deletion via the authenticated delete endpoint and removes account-linked data from core stores used by BarkWise features.

Deletion request page: /web/privacy/delete-account/

6. Permissions

  • Camera: scan invite QR codes and capture/upload community photos.
  • Approximate location: nearby/community and recommendation features.
  • Notifications: send booking, message, and community updates.

7. Security

We use access controls, authentication checks, and rate limiting in the service. You should use production deployments with HTTPS/TLS and secure secrets management.

8. Children

BarkWise is not directed to children under 13.

9. International Processing

Depending on deployment and service providers, data may be processed in countries outside your own jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will update the effective date when changes are made.

11. Contact

Privacy contact: privacy@barkwise.app
