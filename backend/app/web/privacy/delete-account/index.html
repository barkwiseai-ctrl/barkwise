BarkWise

Account Deletion

Effective date: March 7, 2026

You can request deletion of your BarkWise account and associated data using one of the methods below.

Option A: In-App (Recommended)

  1. Open the BarkWise app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile/settings and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm the deletion request.

Option B: Email Request (If You Cannot Access the App)

Email privacy@barkwise.app with subject line “BarkWise Account Deletion Request” and include:

  • Your account user ID (if known)
  • Your invite/login email address
  • Any other identifier that helps us find your account

What Is Deleted

  • Auth/invite/OTP records tied to your account
  • Direct messages and threads tied to your account
  • Notification records and registered push tokens tied to your account
  • Services/bookings/profile listings tied to your account
  • Community posts/comments/events and related records tied to your account
  • AI memory/profile/chat history tied to your account

What May Be Retained Temporarily

  • Security, operational, or legal-compliance logs for a limited period
  • Residual backup data until normal backup rotation completes
